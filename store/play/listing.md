## Full description (max 4000)

Welcome to Petalport, your pocket seaside town!

Make your own character, choose outfits from the boutique, decorate your loft, and explore a colorful town by the sea. Every place is a real little game:

• Dress up: more than 150 clothes and accessories, plus hairstyles and colors to mix and match. Trying on is always free.
• Your home: decorate the Harbour Loft and your bedroom, piece by piece.
• Sunny Café: take orders and build drinks and treats for your customers.
• Cooking: follow recipes for pancakes, crêpes, omelettes and more, and earn stars.
• Town fun: a nail salon, flower stand, skate park, dance stage, fishing, tide pools, a pet shop and a garden that grows.
• Travel: fly to Paris and New York, collect stamps and ride the attractions.
• Photos: take pictures of your character anywhere and keep them in your album.

Made for kids aged 7 to 13, and made to be kind:
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no loot boxes.
• No chat, no strangers, no accounts.
• No timers that punish you: mistakes just show you the right answer.
• Works offline after the first start.
• No personal information is collected. Progress is saved on the device.

All art is original. Have fun in Petalport!
